CONCEPT / SAMPLE

An invitation to explore.

Every game is waiting for the right encounter. This sample shows what a clear invitation should explain: who it is for, when it takes place, how to join, and what happens next.

Illustrative eligibility

  • Aged 18 or over and meeting the activity’s regional requirements.
  • One entry per account, with a verified email address.
  • Illustrative region: unrestricted. Real activities will specify their own regions.

Rules, results and fulfilment

Illustrative prizes: 5 fictional game experience gifts, one per winner. Real activities will specify prizes and eligible regions and use a fixed eligible pool at the deadline.

Results will be anonymised and winners notified on site. An administrator or publisher will fulfil prizes manually and record their status. No Steam password is required and Steam Keys are not automatically stored or dispatched.

A unique email does not establish a unique person. Age and region declarations do not verify identity or location. Real rules require review before launch.
