WHY WE WRITE

Games are vast. Experiences are personal.

Blackwhite is interested in how games are played and how experiences are expressed. Beyond a number, we want to leave a reason; alongside the highlights, we want to make reservations specific.

Editorial judgement and player voices remain distinct. Working with a publisher does not give it control over editorial scores, and discussion should leave room for mutual respect.

01

Play first. Write second.

Ground a point of view in mechanics, levels, stories and the experience of playing.

02

Both sides belong.

Be specific about strengths and limitations, so readers can make their own decision.

03

Make room for discovery.

From independent worlds to niche genres, keep a door open for different tastes.
